Delete your account

You can request deletion from StrandCue. If you cannot access the app, start a request by email.

Effective date: 8 October 2026

  • Delete from the app
  • What deletion removes
  • If you cannot access the app

Delete from the app

  1. Open Settings → Account → Delete account. In the Account area, the button is labelled Delete my account.
  2. Read the deletion information. If prompted, sign out and sign in again for recent authentication.
  3. Type DELETE MY ACCOUNT and choose Delete my account. A reason is optional.
  4. Use Refresh status to check the request. Cancellation is available while deletion is pending, before records are purged.

Once the request is accepted, normal access to your records is blocked. Deletion cannot be undone after purging runs. If you need an export, request it before deletion; contact support if you cannot retrieve it.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes your Hair Passport, chemical services, activities, shelf, tools, history, profile, stored exports, and Auth identity used to sign in.

Non-personal catalogue facts remain. Product and tool catalogue information is separate from your private ownership records.

A restricted deletion record remains for retries and to prevent records from returning after a restore. It includes the account identifier, username, any optional deletion reason, and deletion status and timestamps. Support emails and copies of exports you saved elsewhere are not automatically removed by account deletion. Read the privacy notice's retention details.

If you cannot access the app

Email us from the email address associated with your account and say that you want your StrandCue account deleted. If you no longer have access to that address, explain this without sending sensitive documents.

Never send your password, recovery code, access token, or exported record.

We must verify that the account belongs to you before initiating deletion. Sending an email starts a request; it does not immediately delete the account.

Email an account deletion request

If your device has no email application configured, copy this address into your email service: support@strandcue.co.za. Use the subject “StrandCue account deletion request”.

This website does not delete accounts or accept account credentials. Deletion requests are handled by Common Sense Inc., the operator of StrandCue.
