Support

For help with StrandCue, contact Common Sense Inc. at support@strandcue.co.za. The topics below explain useful first steps.

Effective date: 8 October 2026

  • Sign-in and email confirmation

Sign-in and email confirmation

Sign-in

Use the email address associated with your account. Your private username is not your sign-in address. If you have forgotten your password, use the app's password recovery flow.

Email confirmation

Check the inbox and spam folder for your confirmation email. If the confirmation email is missing or its link cannot be used, contact support at the address below. Do not forward confirmation links.

Password recovery

Start recovery in the app and open the recovery email on the same device. If a link is expired, has already been used, or cannot complete recovery, request a new link. Do not forward recovery links to support.

Export your record

Open Settings → Account → Export my data to request a JSON or CSV export. If asked for recent authentication, sign out and sign in again. Export access expires after 24 hours; request a new export when access has expired.

If an export fails or the app does not let you retrieve the document, email support with the error message, excluding any private record or credentials. Do not attach your exported record.

Account deletion

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Read the account deletion instructions for what is removed and how to request help when you cannot access the app.

Security or privacy concerns

Email support@strandcue.co.za with a brief description of the concern. Share the affected screen or feature and steps to reproduce it, without including another person's information. See the privacy notice for information about access, correction, export, and retention.

Contact support safely

Send your message to support@strandcue.co.za. If no email application opens, copy that address into your email service.

Tell us which feature you were using, what you expected, what happened, and your app version if known. Remove private information from screenshots or error messages before sharing them.

Never send your password, recovery code, access token, or exported record. We need to verify account ownership before acting on a request involving private account information.
