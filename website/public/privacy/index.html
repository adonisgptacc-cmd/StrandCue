Information we use

  • Account details. Supabase Auth handles your email address and authentication information. Your app profile includes a private username, adult eligibility confirmation, country, currency, and temperature preference.
  • Hair records. Your Hair Passport, chemical services, activities, shelf, tools, notes, dates, and history, including revisions and corrections.
  • Operational and security records. Account and request status, timestamps, privacy choices, export job receipts, and deletion records support access checks, reliable processing, and troubleshooting.
  • Support correspondence. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond. Please share only what is needed.

How we use information

We use account details to sign you in and associate your records with your account. Your hair records support current views, history, changes, corrections, and exports. Operational records help protect access, avoid duplicate requests, and complete account deletion.

StrandCue is a cosmetic record of information supplied by you. It does not diagnose and does not prescribe.

Service providers and access

Supabase provides authentication and database services for the app. It processes account information and stores the app records needed to run StrandCue.

Cloudflare hosts this public website, the Android app-link and recovery pages, and the scheduled account deletion worker. That worker communicates with Supabase to complete deletion. This website does not display or accept your hair record.

App access controls restrict private records to the account that owns them. Export and deletion requests require recent authentication. Restricted server operations complete deletion.

Access, correction, and export

You can view your records in the app and use its edit and correction flows to update them. A correction keeps a revision history; it does not erase every earlier entry. Account deletion is a separate action.

You can request an export in Settings → Account → Export my data, choosing JSON or CSV. It includes your profile, Hair Passport and history, chemical services, activities, shelf, tools, and relevant linked catalogue facts. Export access is private to your account and expires after 24 hours. If asked for recent authentication, sign out and sign in again before requesting it.

If you cannot retrieve your export or need help accessing or correcting information, email support@strandcue.co.za. Do not attach your exported record.

Retention and account deletion

Hair records and their revisions stay associated with your account so you can use its history. Archiving a shelf item or tool does not erase its history.

Stored export documents become eligible for cleanup after 7 days; export job receipts remain until account deletion. The 24-hour access expiry applies even before cleanup runs.

Account deletion removes your Hair Passport, services, activities, shelf, tools, history, profile, stored exports, and Auth identity. Non-personal catalogue facts remain. See how to delete your account.

A restricted deletion record remains to support retries and prevent deleted records from returning after a restore. It includes the account identifier, username, any optional deletion reason you supplied, and deletion status and timestamps. Deletion does not automatically remove support emails or copies of exports you saved elsewhere. Contact us about information in support correspondence.

This website and privacy choices

This website has no analytics, advertising, cookies, or contact forms. It uses no third-party scripts and does not collect your hair record. Email links open your own email application.

The app has privacy controls for consent preferences. The current app does not send product analytics or crash reports to an external analytics or crash-reporting provider.

Privacy requests and contact

StrandCue is operated by Common Sense Inc. For questions, access or correction requests, account deletion help, or a privacy concern, email support@strandcue.co.za.

We need to verify that the account belongs to you before acting on requests involving private account information. Never send your password, recovery code, access token, or exported record. If this notice changes, we will update its effective date here.
