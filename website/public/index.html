The StrandCue loop

From what you know to what you can do next.

StrandCue is designed as a continuous private record: prepare with the facts, follow a practical routine, record what really happened and learn from your own observations over time.

  1. 01

    Know your hair

    Build a Hair Passport with your pattern, strand details, length, concerns, chemical services, habits and goals — including “not sure” when that is the honest answer.

  2. 02

    Add what you own

    Keep your products and tools together in My Shelf and My Tools, with exact matches and reviewed facts where available.

  3. 03

    Tell us about today

    Choose the result you want, say how your hair feels now, confirm what is available and answer only the missing questions that matter.

  4. 04

    Review your routine

    Inspect the proposed steps, products, tools, reasons, limitations and alternatives before deciding whether to start.

  5. 05

    Record what happened

    Mark completed, skipped, changed or substituted steps. A plan never becomes a completed activity until you confirm it.

  6. 06

    Notice what works for you

    Use immediate or later Cue Checks to describe how your hair felt and build an honest history with context and sample counts.

Built around real life

Useful guidance starts with your reality.

Person first

A generic hair type never decides a complete routine. Your history, current condition, goals and available time matter.

Owned first

StrandCue is designed to prefer a feasible routine using what you already have and to say when no purchase is needed.

Unknown is valid

Missing information leads to a question, a limitation or a conservative option — never an invented fact.

Exact reviewed facts

Directions and capabilities apply to the reviewed product version and market, with uncertainty kept visible.

Reasons before action

See what a routine is intended to do, why a step appears, and what information is still missing before starting.

Honest personal observations

Your history can show what you reported and how often. It does not claim that a product caused the result.

Private and cosmetic by design

Your record stays yours. The limits stay visible.

StrandCue provides cosmetic hair-care guidance. It does not diagnose and does not prescribe medical treatment.

Planned photos support a private progress record and comparison, not diagnosis, automatic growth measurement or proof of product effectiveness.

Cue Checks are your observations and can show counts and context, but they do not prove that a product caused an outcome.

Privacy controls, export, and account deletion are part of StrandCue. Request an export in the app; if you cannot retrieve it, contact support.

This website has no analytics, advertising, cookies, or contact forms. It does not collect your hair record.
